# F-004: World-Building Persistence

Record NPCs, settlements, locations, factions, and relationships discovered during play. Keep world details in one searchable place.

## What it does

- **Create/edit world details** — add NPCs, settlements, locations, relationships
- **Link to journal entries** — tag NPCs/settlements when you mention them in narrative
- **Quick lookup during play** — see who you've met, where you've been, without scrolling through journal
- **Relationship tracking** — see how characters and locations connect
