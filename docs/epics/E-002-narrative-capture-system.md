# E-002: Narrative Capture System

Your **campaign archive** — a persistent record of every story element you create, so you can pick up your campaign weeks later and remember what you meant.

## Mission

Story emerges from imagination + card flips. But imagination fades. You need a system that captures:
- **What you decided** — your narrative intent after each flip
- **Who you met** — NPCs, their names, relationships, arcs
- **Where you've been** — settlements, locations, world details
- **What happened** — a timeline of sessions and phases

**Success looks like:** You resume a campaign a month later, open the journal, and instantly remember *why* you chose to do that thing, and where the story was heading.

## Scope

This epic covers everything you *record* about a story:

- **Campaign notes** — opening hook, themes, genre, tone
- **Session recaps** — what happened, which phases, key moments
- **Journal entries** — prose/descriptions you write after each flip
- **World details** — settlements, NPCs, locations, factions, relationships
- **Character arc** — your protagonist's journey through the campaign
- **Card history** — which cards have been flipped (optional, for reference)

## What this is NOT

- Not a character sheet (no stats, skills, inventory)
- Not a rules engine (we don't calculate mechanics)
- Not a wiki (we're not trying to be comprehensive; we're trying to be *memorable*)
