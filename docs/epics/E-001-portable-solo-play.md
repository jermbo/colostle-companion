# E-001: Portable Solo Play

Solo tabletop narrative RPG in your pocket. Replace three physical books, a card deck, and a notebook with one app so you can play Colostle anywhere—park bench, lunch break, flight.

## Mission

Today you need: (1) three reference books, (2) a shuffled card deck, (3) something to write with. Tomorrow you need your phone.

**Success looks like:** You pick up the app on a park bench with zero setup, flip a card, read a prompt, journal your choice, and feel the story moving forward.

## Constraints & scope

- **Solo play only** — no multiplayer, no GM complexity. Just you, the deck, and imagination.
- **Everything in the app** — no flipping between books and app, no physical cards required. The app *is* Colostle for this player.
- **Narrative-first, stat-light** — we don't track inventory, equipment, or character sheets. We track intent: what you wanted to happen, what you discovered, who you met.
- **Open-ended campaigns** — no win condition, no time limit. Player decides when the story ends.
