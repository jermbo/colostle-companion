# Epics — Why We're Building This

Three interlocking missions define the Colostle Companion:

## [E-001: Portable Solo Play](./E-001-portable-solo-play.md)
Everything needed to play Colostle on your phone. No books, no cards, no notebook—just imagination and the app. Play anywhere: park bench, lunch break, commute.

## [E-002: Narrative Capture System](./E-002-narrative-capture-system.md)
Your campaign archive. Journal entries, NPCs, settlements, world details, narrative intent—a persistent record so you remember what you meant when you pick it back up weeks later.

## [E-003: Instant Oracle Lookup](./E-003-instant-oracle-lookup.md)
The core mechanic. Flip a card, instantly see the prompt. No book-fumbling, no table cross-referencing. Three seconds from flip to story moment.

---

## How they fit together

**E-001** (portable) is the **container** — everything in one app.
**E-003** (oracle) is the **engine** — the card-to-prompt mechanism.
**E-002** (narrative) is the **memory** — what you capture to remember intent.

Together, they answer the core question: *Why play Colostle on an app instead of physically?*

**Answer:** Because it's faster, you remember your intent, and you can play anywhere.
